# Boolean and Collection stats dedupe at derivation, not at write

Boolean stats count distinct marked days ("worked out 10 of the last 30 days") and Collection stats count distinct named items ("visited 5 of 195 countries"), so both invite a uniqueness constraint — one entry per (stat, day), one per (stat, item). We deliberately have none: duplicate entries are accepted everywhere and collapsed when aggregates are derived (Boolean dedupes by local day key, Collection by trimmed case-folded item name). Write-time uniqueness cannot be guaranteed anyway — the offline queue is append-only and flushed as plain creates (ADR-0001), so two devices marking the same day offline both land. Since totals are always derived and never stored, deduplicating in the aggregation layer makes duplicates harmless by construction instead of a conflict to resolve. The cost is that raw entries may contain redundant rows a reader might mistake for data corruption; they are not.
